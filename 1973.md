The 1973 Lawsuit Overview
  - Filing Date: October 15, 1973 (United States v. Fred C. Trump, Donald Trump, and Trump Management, Inc., Docket 1:73-01529, E.D.N.Y.).
  - Core Allegations: The DOJ charged that the Trumps systematically discriminated against Black applicants by telling them apartments were unavailable while offering leases to white applicants, imposing harsher rental terms, and marking Black applicants' forms with a "C" (for "colored").
  - Defense & Countersuit: Represented by attorney Roy Cohn, Donald Trump denied the charges and filed a $100 million countersuit against the federal government for defamation, which was later dismissed.
  - The Settlement: In June 1975, the parties signed a consent decree requiring the Trumps to furnish weekly lists of vacancies to the Urban League, advertise openings in minority-focused newspapers, and obey fair housing laws without admitting any guilt.
  - Follow-up Action: The DOJ sued the Trumps again in 1978 for continuing racial steering and non-compliance before the case was finally closed in 1982.
